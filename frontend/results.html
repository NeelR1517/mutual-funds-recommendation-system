    About the benchmark

    The market series is an equal-weight composite of Nifty 50, Nifty 500, Midcap 150 and Smallcap 250 index funds. It carries fund expenses and tracking error, so it is a fair comparison, not an official index level.

    04 Monte Carlo projection

    A range of outcomes, not a forecast.

    The simulation resamples the portfolio’s recent monthly returns and applies your SIP plan. The spread shows how much the ending value depends on the order of returns.

    What the data does not contain

    Read me
    • No AUM, expense ratio, manager or holdings. mfapi.in does not publish them, so the score cannot judge size, cost or style drift.
    • No official index TRI. Benchmarks are equal-weight composites of index funds with tracking error and expenses.
    • Survivorship bias. Only live schemes appear in the feed.
    • Inferred categories. Labels come from fund names and category text.
    • Past returns do not persist. A high score is evidence about the past, not a promise about the future.
