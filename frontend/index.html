Method — no black boxes

Everything above is computed from public data with documented formulas. Here's the full pipeline.

1 · Risk score

Four questionnaire inputs → sub-scores → weighted sum: 0.35·horizon + 0.30·appetite + 0.20·age + 0.15·SIP. Horizon is capped by years to retirement. Full breakdown is always displayed above.

2 · Features

NAV history per fund (mfapi.in → AMFI): CAGR 1/3/5Y, annualised σ·√252, Sharpe = (CAGR₃ − 7%) / σ, max drawdown 5Y, % positive rolling monthly windows.

3 · K-means cohorts

Clusters on standardised log volatility (w 2), |max drawdown| (1.5), CAGR₃ (0.7), consistency (0.4), k = 5, seeded for reproducibility. Cohorts ordered by mean volatility.

4 · RandomForest cross-check

150 bagged trees (depth ≤ 4) predict the fund's SEBI Riskometer level (1–6) from behaviour. Leave-one-out CV accuracy shown; each fund card displays the model's vote, confidence, and agreement with the official label.

5 · Weighted ranking

Inside your cohort, funds rank by Σ wᵢ·scoreᵢ where sub-scores are winsorised min-max normalisations of the 5 components — and all five weights are yours to adjust live.

6 · SIP backtest

Monthly investments replayed on real NAVs through 1/3/5-year windows — basket vs a Nifty 50 index fund proxy, reporting XIRR (bisection IRR) and max drawdown of the value path.

Data snapshot

Honest limitations

  • TER & AUM are curated approximations (dated, from public sources) — mfapi.in does not expose them. Replace with AMFI scheme-attributes for production accuracy.
  • The SEBI Riskometer label is reconstructed at category level; each fund's real label can differ and is dynamic.
  • The Nifty 50 benchmark is proxied by — an index fund NAV, not the official TRI.
  • 72 funds is a curated universe, not the whole market; plans are Direct-Growth only.
  • Past performance analysis is descriptive. Nothing here predicts the future.
SahiMF — decision support, not decision replacement. It filters, matches, ranks and explains; the final call stays with the investor.
