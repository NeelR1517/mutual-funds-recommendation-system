01 What this dashboard answers

Four questions, one explainable plan.

The prototype’s separate analysis pages are folded into one readable flow: what was screened, why each fund scores well, how the money is split, and what history says about the result.

Fund quality

Is this fund good — for its category?

Nine return, risk, consistency and momentum metrics are percentile-ranked inside large, mid, small, flexi and index. A small-cap fund is compared with small caps, never with a large-cap fund.

Suitability

How much equity belongs in my plan?

Age, horizon and risk tolerance set an equity target with hard caps for short horizons. The debt / cash gap stays explicit because this universe contains equity funds only.

Optimizer

Which funds get how much weight?

Up to two funds per category enter a long-only optimizer with 3–30% position bounds, category bands and a volatility budget. You see the final weights and the target mix side by side.

Evidence

What happened in the past?

A five-year monthly replay compares the equity sleeve with a Nifty 500 proxy. Two thousand Monte Carlo paths then show the range of possible SIP outcomes — not a forecast.

03 How the plan is built

From questionnaire to tested portfolio.

The same four steps appear on your results page, filled in with your numbers.

01 · Profile

Answer four questions

Age, investment horizon, monthly SIP and comfort with drawdowns. The answers map to a risk tolerance and a suitability target.

02 · Score

Rank inside the category

Each metric becomes a category percentile. Missing metrics — usually a 5-year record for a young fund — are dropped rather than filled.

03 · Optimize

Fit the portfolio

The optimizer picks up to two funds per category and respects position limits, category bands and a risk-based volatility cap.

04 · Test

Replay and project

The final sleeve is replayed over monthly NAV history against a Nifty 500 proxy, then resampled 2,000 times to show a range of outcomes.

Read this before using the numbers

This is research and decision support, not investment advice. The quality score is not a return forecast. Benchmarks are equal-weight index-fund proxies, not official TRI values, and the data carries survivorship bias because only live schemes are listed. Past performance does not predict future returns.
